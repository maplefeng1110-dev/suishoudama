常见问题

怎么给截图打码？

在 App 里选「选图打码」选一张截图，用手指拖动框选要遮挡的区域，选马赛克或纯色块，然后保存/拷贝/分享。也可以截图后在系统分享面板里点「随手打码」直接打码。

怎么把多张截图拼成长图？

选「长截图拼接」→ 多选截图 → 点选要拼的、可调整顺序 → 「拼接」。会自动检测重叠并去重。

"录屏转长图"怎么用？

iOS 不允许第三方 App 自动滚动别的 App，所以请打开系统录屏、边匀速慢滚边录制你要保存的聊天/页面，停止后回到 App，会自动把录屏拼成一张长图。

我的图片会被上传吗？

不会。全程在你的设备本地处理，App 不联网、不上传、不收集任何数据。导出还会自动去除 EXIF/GPS 等元数据。

需要注册账号吗？

不需要。打开即用，无需登录。

FAQ

How do I censor a screenshot?

Choose "Censor a Photo", pick a screenshot, drag to select the area to hide, choose mosaic or a solid block, then save / copy / share. You can also tap "Snap Censor" in the system share sheet right after taking a screenshot.

How do I stitch multiple screenshots?

Choose "Stitch Long Screenshot" → multi-select screenshots → pick which to include and reorder → Stitch. Overlaps are detected and removed automatically.

How does "Recording → Long Image" work?

iOS doesn't let third-party apps auto-scroll other apps, so start the system screen recording, scroll slowly and steadily through the chat/page, stop, and return to the app — it stitches the recording into one long image.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Everything is processed on your device. The app has no network access, uploads nothing, and collects no data. Exports also strip EXIF/GPS metadata.

Do I need an account?

No. It works immediately, no sign-in.
