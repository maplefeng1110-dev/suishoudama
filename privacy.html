隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-10-24

随手打码是一个截图打码、长图拼接工具，设计上把隐私放在第一位：你的数据全部留在你自己的设备上。

App 访问什么

  • 相册 —— 仅用于读取你主动选择的截图/录屏，以及在你点击保存后把结果写入相册；不会访问你未选择的内容。
  • 屏幕录制（"录屏转长图"功能） —— 仅用于把你自己录制的滚动录屏拼接成长图。录屏只在本机处理，绝不上传。
  • 通知 —— 仅用于在录屏结束时提醒你回到 App。它是设备本地发出的通知，不经过任何服务器；不允许也不影响使用。

在设备本地存储哪些数据

  • 仅你在 App 内打开或生成的图片/录屏；处理后由你选择是否保存到相册。
  • 少量本地偏好设置（如默认马赛克强度），仅存于设备本地。

数据如何处理

  • 所有处理都完全在你的设备本地完成。
  • 我们不会上传、传输、出售或分享你的数据。 App 没有服务器，也不会为你的数据发起任何网络连接。
  • 不需要账号、不需要登录、不收集任何个人身份信息。
  • 不含任何第三方 SDK、不做分析统计、无广告、无追踪。
  • 导出的图片不带 EXIF/GPS 等任何元数据。

你的控制权

  • 所有结果是否保存由你决定；不保存即不留存。
  • 删除 App 会一并移除设备上存储的所有内容。

权限

相册、屏幕录制、通知等权限会在首次使用相关功能时请求；你可随时在 iOS「设置」中撤销。

儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集其数据。

政策变更

我们可能更新本政策，届时将更新上方的生效日期。

Snap Censor is a screenshot mosaic & long-screenshot tool. It is designed to keep your data private: everything stays on your device.

What the app accesses

  • Photos — only to read screenshots/recordings you explicitly select, and to save results to your library when you tap save. It never accesses content you did not choose.
  • Screen recording ("Recording → Long Image") — only to stitch your own scrolling screen recording into one long image. The recording is processed on-device and never uploaded.
  • Notifications — only to bring you back to the app when a screen recording finishes. They are generated locally on your device with no server involved, and everything works without them.

What is stored (on your device only)

  • Only the images/recordings you open or create in the app; results are saved to Photos only if you choose to.
  • A few local preferences (e.g. default mosaic strength), stored on-device.

How your data is handled

  • All processing happens entirely on your device.
  • We do not upload, transmit, sell, or share your data. The app has no servers and makes no network connections with your data.
  • No account, no sign-in, and no personal identifiers are collected.
  • No third-party SDKs, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Exported images carry no EXIF/GPS or other metadata.

Your control

  • You decide whether any result is saved; nothing is kept otherwise.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.

Permissions

Photos, screen-recording and notification permissions are requested when you first use the related feature; you can revoke them anytime in iOS Settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change accordingly.
